# 00 · Brand Overview

> The "why" behind every design decision. If a choice can't be traced back to this page, question it.

## Purpose
**Make it simple. Make it yours.**
We exist to help people do more with less — removing friction so they can focus on what matters.

## Positioning
A people-led, technology-powered company dedicated to helping people make it simple and make it theirs.

## Audience
| Segment | Who | What they need from us |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Small teams and independent professionals | Tools that work out of the box, no manual |
| Secondary | Enterprise buyers | Confidence, reliability, clear governance |
| Internal | Employees and partners | Consistent, easy-to-apply brand assets |

## Brand Principles
These are internal filters — not taglines. Every touchpoint should pass all three.

| Principle | Meaning | In practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Grounded** | Relatable, down-to-earth, part of the community | Plain language, real photography, no hype |
| **Agile** | Constantly improving, always adapting | Fast motion, lightweight UI, ship-and-learn |
| **Pioneering** | We blaze a path and never settle | Bold colour, confident type, unexpected but useful details |

## Personality
If Rectio were a person: **a sharp, friendly expert** — knows their stuff, explains it simply, never talks down.

- We are: clear, warm, confident, optimistic
- We are not: corporate, cold, clever-for-its-own-sake, loud

## Brand Architecture
- **Master brand:** Rectio (mark + wordmark)
- **Sub-brands / products:** Rectio + descriptor lockup (see `02-logo.md`). No separate logos.
- **Co-branding:** Rectio lockup first, divider, partner logo at equal optical weight.

## One-line brief
> Bold indigo and coral, generous rounded cards, light-weight display type, plain-spoken copy.
